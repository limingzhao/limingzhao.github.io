Zailex privacy policy

Zailex is a private assistant used by a single family. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar, read-only. It reads events from the family's shared calendar. It cannot create, change or delete events.
  • Gmail, send-only. It can send email from the assistant's own account to family members. It cannot read, search or delete mail.

Where the data goes

Calendar data is stored only on the family's own computer at home. All processing, including any AI model, runs on that computer. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any outside AI service.

Retention and removal

Data is kept on the family's computer for as long as the family uses the assistant and can be deleted by them at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings.

Contact

Questions go to the family member who operates the assistant.
